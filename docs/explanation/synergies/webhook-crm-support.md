# Synergy: webhook → CRM + support

**Domains:** `webhooks.*`, `crm.*`, `support.*`

Lead capture recipes upsert CRM contacts from inbound webhooks. Support threads (`psup_*`) stay on the messenger plane — shown read-only on Contact 360, not as CRM entities.
